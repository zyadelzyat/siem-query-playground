# Dataset attribution

The 15 imported log datasets were supplied by the project owner from [Security Datasets](https://securitydatasets.com/introduction.html), an initiative by Roberto Rodriguez and Jose Luis Rodriguez / Open Threat Research Forge.

The original public dataset collection is maintained in [OTRF/Security-Datasets](https://github.com/OTRF/Security-Datasets). Source record contents are preserved; this project adds normalized training fields, compressed delivery, and exercises. The source files themselves remain unchanged.
